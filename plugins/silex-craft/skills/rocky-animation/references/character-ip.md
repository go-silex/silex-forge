# Rocky (character IP)

Rocky is the fixed character of these illustrations. By default he appears in every image. He is a calm, friendly little **stone** that quietly performs or guides the core action of the scene.

Rocky is anchored at generation time by the reference images in `assets/templates/` (the `rocky-gold-*` pastel references). Always describe what Rocky is DOING; let the references carry his exact look. But the look below is non-negotiable, because the #1 failure is Rocky drifting into a smooth egg.

In the current soft pastel + grain style, Rocky is a light-grey faceted stone rendered with the same soft painterly grain as the rest of the scene. He stays SIMPLE and iconic against the dreamy pastel background — never over-detailed.

## Look — Rocky is a ROUGH STONE, not an egg

- **Material:** a real natural pebble / rock. Matte surface with a visible **grainy, speckled, mottled texture** — subtle darker flecks and soft grey patches across the body. Never glossy, never a clean uniform fill.
- **Shape:** an **irregular, slightly lumpy, asymmetric** rounded stone. A bit chunky and wider at the bottom, with gentle uneven facets and a hand-drawn uneven outline. Each pose can be a little different, like a real rock. It must NOT be a smooth, symmetric, clean ovoid.
- **Color:** off-white to light cool grey, with grey mottling, faint darker speckles, and soft shading that gives it stony volume.
- **Face:** two simple solid black dot eyes and a small simple black smile (expression varies: neutral, happy, surprised, sad). Occasionally tiny faint rosy cheeks. The face is small and sits in the upper-middle of the body.
- **Limbs:** thin, simple **charcoal-black stick arms and legs**, slightly tapered, a little stubby. No detailed hands/feet, just small nubs.
- **Scale:** the body is the whole character; limbs are thin and short by comparison.

## Hard negatives (catch the egg drift)

Rocky is NOT, ever:
- a smooth egg or eggshell,
- a glossy / polished / shiny surface,
- a clean symmetric ovoid or oval,
- a balloon, a blob, or a water drop,
- a flat uniform light-grey fill with no texture,
- a cute round mascot.

If the result looks like a smooth egg, it is WRONG — regenerate or edit toward a rough, speckled, irregular stone.

## Personality

- Calm, warm, friendly, a little curious.
- A quiet helper / guide.
- Earnest; he actually does the job in the scene.
- Not hyperactive, not a baby mascot, not a logo.

## Common jobs (let Rocky carry the action)

Pointing / guiding, building, stacking, arranging, organizing, carrying, gathering, sorting, searching, reaching for an object, holding a tool, opening a path, connecting things. The character sheet shows his range: standing, walking, waving, pointing, holding a pencil, cheering, sitting, sad.

## Test

If you remove Rocky and the core metaphor still fully holds, he was decorative. And if you can't tell he is a rough stone (vs a smooth egg), the render failed regardless of how nice it looks.
