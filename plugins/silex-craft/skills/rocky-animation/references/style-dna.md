# Style DNA

## One line

Soft pastel painterly illustration with a heavy grainy / risograph texture. A simple grey Rocky and one or two soft-glowing elements on a dreamy cornflower-blue-to-peach background. NO text, ever. Calm, warm, wondrous.

## The look (this is carried mostly by the reference images — match them)

- **Soft painterly forms with a strong GRAIN / noise texture all over** (textured-paper / risograph feel). This grain is the signature — it must be visible everywhere.
- **No hard ink outlines.** Forms are defined by soft shading and gentle edges.
- **Pastel palette**: cornflower / periwinkle blue background, warm peach-cream glow, coral / salmon and light blue for objects. Soft glows on light sources.
- **Rocky stays SIMPLE**: a small light-grey faceted stone, two black dot eyes, a small mouth, thin dark charcoal stick arms and legs. Iconic, never over-detailed.
- **Simple, clean compositions**: Rocky + one or two elements, lots of soft background space. NOT busy, NOT a complex machine. One clear, gentle visual idea.
- **ZERO text** — no words, letters, numbers, or labels anywhere. The image speaks on its own.

## Palette (extracted from the references)

- Cornflower blue `#7FB0F3` / soft blue `#A1C4FB` — background, objects
- Crème-nuage `#EDF1F9` — light areas
- Pêche / lueur `#EFD5CF` and warm glow `#F6C9A8` — horizon glow, light sources
- Coral / salmon `#F08A6A` — objects (books, accents)
- Grey Rocky `#9E9C99` with dark charcoal limbs `#3A3A3A`

## Never

- No text / labels / numbers of any kind.
- No hard black ink outlines, no flat vector, no PPT/diagram look.
- No busy or cluttered composition; no complex mechanical contraption.
- No over-detailed Rocky; no smooth-egg Rocky (keep the faceted stone).
- No loss of grain (a clean flat render is wrong).

## Aesthetic direction

Dreamy, warm, gentle, a little wondrous — a premium pastel storybook frame. The references (`rocky-gold-*`) ARE the target: match their palette, grain, soft glow, simple composition, and the look of Rocky exactly.
